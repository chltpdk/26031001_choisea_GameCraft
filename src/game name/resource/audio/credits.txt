DEAD ZONE — After the Sirens
Original synthesized instrumental intro theme created for this project.
80 BPM, E minor, 16 bars / 48 seconds, seamless stereo loop.
Low heartbeat percussion, pulsing bass, dark pads, and sparse bell melody.
No third-party recordings, samples, or soundfonts used.

Created with a custom C# synthesizer. The generator was removed during
project cleanup; the finished WAV is retained as a game resource.

The main menu fades in at 65% volume. START and focus loss fade out.
Returning to the main menu restarts the theme. Gameplay has no intro music.

DEAD ZONE — Apocalypse (dead_zone_apocalypse.wav)
Original synthesized gameplay music created for this project.
Stereo PCM (44.1 kHz / 16 bit), 19.642857 seconds including a one-time musical opening.
The repeated 6-second intro excerpt has been removed; the track starts with the arranged bridge.
Related bell, bass and minor-chord phrases connect it through 96 and 112 BPM
to the established bright 128 BPM gameplay passage. No opening filter sweep or volume swell.
The preserved E-C-D-B gameplay passage repeats for 15 seconds from sample 204750;
the intro-themed opening does not repeat during uninterrupted gameplay.
No third-party recordings, samples, or soundfonts used. Only the finished WAV is retained.
Starts after the menu intro fades out on START; loops during gameplay.
Pause and game over reduce its volume; focus loss fades it out.

Gunshot (gunshot.wav)
Original synthesized short gunshot: 125 ms mono PCM, 44.1 kHz / 16 bit.
Soft crack, low body and brief mechanical tail; no external recordings or samples.
Plays at 22% volume on each actual shot, including immediate muzzle impacts.
The effect is shorter than the 160 ms firing interval to keep automatic fire clean.

Zombie death (zombie_death.wav)
Original synthesized low death groan: 360 ms mono PCM, 44.1 kHz / 16 bit.
Falling low vocal register, irregular vocal fry and a rough breath tail replace the high squeal.
No external recordings or samples. Plays quietly at 18% volume only on a confirmed kill.
Three playback voices allow quick successive deaths to overlap without cutting off the cry.
